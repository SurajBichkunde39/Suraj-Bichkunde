Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Welcome to Morse Lab, a Morse code translator for Android and iOS, developed by Suraj Bichkunde (Awakened). Your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy explains how we handle user information.

Information We Collect

Morse Lab does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDKs and no third-party tracking.

Internet Usage and Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions and has no network access. It works fully offline.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your sound and notation preferences in a small settings file in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device.

When you export text or WAV audio files, they are created in the app's private cache. They leave your device only when you explicitly share them or save them to a location you choose using the system share sheet or file picker. Once shared or saved, that data is governed by the app or service you pick.

Deleting Your Data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes all of the data described above. Since no personal data is collected, there is nothing to access, export or delete beyond uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

Morse Lab is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page with a new effective date.
