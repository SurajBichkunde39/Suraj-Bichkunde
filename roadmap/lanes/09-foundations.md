# 09 · Foundations (always on)

**Status:** Always on, low dose — a little every month, never a full season

## Why this matters for you

These are the things that don't belong to any one technology but decide how
far the technologies take you: thinking in systems, communicating clearly,
being hireable on short notice, and not shipping security holes. They're also
the things that were **missing from the original list** — worth adding
deliberately.

## Habits (not milestones — keep them running)

- **Write in public — ~1 article/month** on this site
  (`src/content/articles/`). Most lane milestones already end in an article;
  publish those. Use `series:` in frontmatter for multi-part topics.
- **System design — 1 practice per month.** Alternate between writing a design
  doc for a system you know and one you don't.
- **Interview readiness — keep it warm, not hot.** ~2 DSA problems a week is
  enough to never be caught cold. Update the resume/site after every season.
- **Community.** You restarted the Android meetup and spoke at DroidTribe.
  Aim for ~2 talks a year; most milestones here are talk material.

## Milestones

- [ ] **FD1 — Design doc: the BookMyShow seat-booking + payments flow** (from
  memory, no confidential details): requirements, APIs, data model,
  consistency, failure modes. *Artifact:* article.
- [ ] **FD2 — Design doc: SDUI Lab.** Write it *before* BE3. Revisit after.
  *Artifact:* doc in the SDUI Lab repo.
- [ ] **FD3 — Security refresh.** OWASP Mobile Top 10 + API Security Top 10;
  audit one of your apps and SDUI Lab against them. Your infosec internship
  is a head start. *Artifact:* findings + fixes.
- [ ] **FD4 — A talk.** Turn the best milestone of the season into a talk
  (meetup or internal). Mockcat MCP (AG5) is a strong candidate.
  *Artifact:* slides + recording link.
- [ ] **FD5 — Mock interview round.** Once a year, do a full mock loop
  (DSA, system design, mobile architecture, behavioral) to see where you stand.
  *Artifact:* a list of gaps → fed into the next season.
